Kriston.AI

Mar – Sep 2026

LLM Post-Training Engineer · Intern

Training for multi-turn conversations

I worked on healthcare lead-capture models, from two-stage supervised fine-tuning to a production feedback loop for finding failures, repairing responses, and retraining. For the paediatric assistant, this also included DPO and multi-teacher on-policy distillation.

Evaluation failure rate: 25% → 5% on a rolling set of 1,000 real production conversations.

Agentic RL, grounded in the production workflow

I recreated the agent environment, built a user simulator, and combined turn-level and trajectory-level rewards for credit assignment across long conversations. I customised verl’s multi-turn rollouts and GRPO training loop, then audited the resulting trajectories for simulator and reward biases.

Internal Excellent-Bench: 20% → 70% alignment with expert sales behaviour. The model entered a phased rollout serving 100,000+ calls per day.

Agents that follow up, remember, and act
